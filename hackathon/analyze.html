What we found

AI result
  • Dark circular spots across the leaf surface, concentrated along veins.
  • Yellowing halo around lesions consistent with fungal leaf blight.
  • Localized damage — most of the leaf still shows healthy green tissue.

Image-based identification can be uncertain. Confirm with a local certified agronomist before applying chemical treatments or making costly interventions.

Recommended action

  1. 1

    Remove heavily affected leaves. Prune the most spotted foliage and discard it away from healthy plants to reduce spread.

  2. 2

    Keep foliage dry where possible. Avoid overhead watering; water at the base to reduce prolonged leaf wetness.

  3. 3

    Monitor nearby plants for similar symptoms. Check surrounding crops in 3–5 days and repeat analysis if new spots appear.
